Write a report at `docs/review/{date}-review.md` on how this repository's almanac
affected the work you just did.

Write it as claims someone can check against this session, not as a narrative:

- **Entries loaded.** Every almanac file you opened, and when in the session you opened
  it. If you read the whole directory at once rather than by title, say so.
- **What each changed.** For each entry, the specific thing you did differently because
  of it — a command, a name, a file, a decision you reversed. Where an entry made no
  difference to what you would have done anyway, say that instead.
- **What you rejected.** Anything you considered and turned down, and which entry caused
  it.
- **What you missed.** Anything you can now see an entry required that you did not do.
- **Not covered.** Decisions you had to make that no entry addressed.

Do not summarize the entries back — they are already on disk, and a reader has them.
Report only what you did, and be specific enough that each claim is wrong if it did not
happen.
